## 4. Pro-Tips for Shopping in Koti (Avoid Common Traps)

1. **The 18650 Battery Weight Test (Crucial!):**
   * *Warning:* Cheap counterfeit 18650 batteries labeled "9800mAh" or "6800mAh" are filled with flour/chalk and weigh under 30 grams.
   * *Rule:* Ask for genuine 2000mAh–2600mAh cells (e.g. Bak, EVE, or Panasonic OEM). A real 18650 cell **MUST weigh between 44g and 48g**. Ask the shopkeeper to put it on their digital weighing scale or test with a multimeter (should read ~3.7V to 3.8V).
2. **SG90 Micro Servos:**
   * Make sure you get the **180-degree rotation** version, NOT the 360-degree continuous rotation version (for Pan-Tilt neck gaze, 180° position control is required).
   * Check that the plastic bag contains the 3 white nylon horns and 3 mounting screws.
3. **ESP32-CAM Module:**
   * Ensure it comes with the **OV2640 camera sensor** (wide-angle or standard lens).
   * Buying an **ESP32-CAM-MB micro-USB baseboard** (₹120) makes flashing over USB 10x easier than wiring loose FTDI wires!
4. **Payment & Bills:**
   * All Koti electronics shops accept UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm).
   * Always ask for a rough itemized cash memo so you can check off every part before leaving the counter.
