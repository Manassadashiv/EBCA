📍 Koti Component Shops to Visit for Quotes

  • Gujarati Galli (Main electronics lane):
    • Universal Electronics / Universal Components: Best for ESP32, ToF sensors, and pre-soldered module breakouts. Ask for Mr. Ramesh/counter manager.
    • Sagar Electronics / Sagar Components: Excellent competitive pricing on sensors, buck converters, and multiplexers.
    • Metro Electronics / Shweta Electronics: Great for 18650 cells, battery cases, chargers, breadboards, and jumper ribbons.
  • Troop Bazaar (Hardware & Motors):
    • Vishal Electronics & Pioneer Electronics: Best for 2WD chassis kits, BO gearmotors, rubber wheels, casters, brass spacers & screws.
  • How to Walk the Market: Start at Gujarati Galli (Bank Street entrance) → survey electronics/sensors → walk through to Troop Bazaar for chassis & motors.

📝 How to Request an "Estimation Slip"

  • College Requirement Pretext: Always say: "Bhaiya, guide/college approval ke liye quotation estimate chahiye, sanction aane pe poora set yahi se khareedenge."
  • They Will Write it on a Memo: Most shops will write down individual rates on their small printed rough memo slip with their shop stamp/visiting card!
  • Collect Visiting Cards: Take the shop's visiting card and staple/attach their rough estimate slip so you can compare prices at home tonight.
  • Ask About Package Discount: Always ask: "Agar 16 items ka complete package lein, toh kitna kam karoge?" (Usually 5% to 10% lower than individual items).
  • Take Multimeter for Reference: If you have a small multimeter, keep it in your bag to verify batteries when you return to buy.
