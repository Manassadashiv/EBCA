### 3.1 Multi-Hazard Adversarial Stress Models

### 3.2 Causal Recovery Time & Statistical Necessity
The distress recovery duration $\tau_{\text{recovery}}$ is defined as the elapsed time from hazard onset $t_0$ until all 7 introspective distress telemetry channels return below nominal thresholds:
$$\tau_{\text{recovery}} = \inf \left\{ t > t_0 \;\middle|\; \max_{c \in [1, 7]} d_c(t) \le d_{\text{nominal}, c} \right\} - t_0$$
For paired trials $i \in [1, 40]$ across Intact and Ablated conditions, the recovery advantage is evaluated using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test on paired differences $D_i = \tau_{\text{ablated}, i} - \tau_{\text{intact}, i}$:
$$W = \sum_{i=1}^{40} \text{rank}(|D_i|) \cdot \operatorname{sgn}(D_i)$$
